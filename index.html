How to play

Find groups of four items that share something in common.

  • Select four tiles, then hit Submit to check.
  • Find the four groups without making four mistakes.
  • Get three of four right and we'll tell you you're one away.

Category difficulty

  • Sage — one extra step
  • Slate — needs some CanCon knowledge
  • Clay — wordplay or deep cut
